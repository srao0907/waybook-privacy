Waybook Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your name, email and (if you add one) profile photo, to give you an account.
  • The trips you plan: destinations, stays, activities, costs, checklists, documents, chat messages, journal entries and photos.
  • Your location, only while you use the Emergency page or if you turn on “Share my location” for a trip.
  • Emergency contacts you choose to add.

Why

Only to run Waybook: to sign you in, save your trips, and share them with the people you invite. Waybook has no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and never sells your data.

Who can see it

Trip information is visible to the members of that trip. Viewers only see what the trip owner allows. Private journal entries and photos are shown only to you. Your friends list is only visible to you.

Where it’s stored

On Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), protected by security rules so only trip members can read a trip. Weather comes from Open-Meteo and exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API; they only receive a place or currency, never who you are. Maps, directions and translation use Apple’s services.

Your choices

Location, Calendar, Photos and notifications are only used if you allow them, and can be turned off in Settings at any time. You can block anyone in a trip, and report messages, journal entries or photos from their menu.

Deleting your data

In the app, Profile → Delete account removes your account and your data: your journal entries, photos, messages, emergency contact, shared location, friends and personal checklist. Trips you own with others are handed to another member; solo trips are deleted.
